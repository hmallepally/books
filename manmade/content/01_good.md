# Good: Human Inventions

## Wheel

Image: ../images/wheel.png

The wheel is one of humanity's earliest inventions. It reduces friction when moving heavy loads, enabling transport and machinery. Mechanically, a wheel converts rotational motion to linear movement when paired with an axle.

## Printing Press

Image: ../images/printing_press.png

The printing press enabled mass communication by mechanically transferring inked type to paper. It dramatically lowered the cost of books and spread ideas.
