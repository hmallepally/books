# Good Inventions — Expanded

## Agriculture

Image: ../images/agriculture.png

Domestication of plants and animals allowed humans to settle, store surplus food, and support larger populations. Agriculture created the basis for civilization by enabling specialization and cities.

## The Wheel

Image: ../images/wheel.png

By turning friction-limited sliding motion into rolling motion, wheels enabled efficient transport and machines like carts and gears.

## Writing and Record-Keeping

Image: ../images/writing.png

Systems of writing (cuneiform, hieroglyphs, alphabets) enabled record-keeping, laws, literature, and science by allowing information to persist across time and space.

## The Printing Press

Image: ../images/printing_press.png

Mechanized reproduction of text made books cheaper and spread ideas quickly across societies, accelerating education and reform.

## Electricity and the Electric Generator

Image: ../images/electricity.png

Harnessing electricity powered lighting, communication, and machines. Generators convert mechanical energy to electrical energy via electromagnetic induction.

## Vaccination

Image: ../images/vaccination.png

Vaccines train the immune system to recognize pathogens, preventing disease and saving millions of lives. They rely on controlled exposure to antigens.

## Steam Engine

Image: ../images/steam_engine.png

Steam engines converted heat into mechanical work and powered factories, trains, and ships — central to the Industrial Revolution.

## The Telephone

Image: ../images/telephone.png

Converts sound into electrical signals and back, enabling near-instant voice communication over long distances.

## The Computer

Image: ../images/computer.png

Programmable machines that process and store data. Computers automate calculation, control systems, and information workflows.

## The Internet

Image: ../images/internet.png

A global network of networks that connects computers and people, enabling rapid information exchange, services, and new economies.

## Antibiotics

Image: ../images/antibiotics.png

Drugs like penicillin kill or inhibit bacteria and transformed medical care by treating infections that were once fatal.

## Refrigeration

Image: ../images/refrigeration.png

Preserving food by slowing microbial growth — enabling long-distance transport and reducing spoilage.

## Sanitation and Sewage Systems

Image: ../images/sanitation.png

Removing waste and providing clean water vastly reduced infectious disease and improved urban living standards.

## Air Travel (Aeroplane)

Image: ../images/airplane.png

Powered flight connected the globe quickly, enabling rapid travel, trade, and cultural exchange.

## Renewable Energy (Solar, Wind)

Image: ../images/renewable.png

Technologies that convert sunlight and wind into electricity help reduce dependence on fossil fuels and lower emissions.
