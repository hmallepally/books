# Bad Inventions — Expanded

## Asbestos Insulation

Image: ../images/asbestos.png

Used for heat resistance and insulation; inhaled fibers cause lung disease and cancers.

## Leaded Gasoline

Image: ../images/leaded_gasoline.png

Added to improve engine performance but released toxic lead into the environment causing neurological damage, especially in children.

## Chemical Weapons

Image: ../images/chemical_weapons.png

Designed to cause mass harm through toxins. They produce long-term health, environmental, and moral consequences.

## Thalidomide (as medicine)

Image: ../images/thalidomide.png

Prescribed as a sedative and to treat morning sickness in the 1950s; it caused severe birth defects in thousands of children before being pulled from the market.

## Agent Orange

Image: ../images/agent_orange.png

Herbicide used in warfare that caused long-term health issues and environmental damage due to toxic dioxins.

## Single-Use Plastics

Image: ../images/single_use_plastics.png

Convenient for packaging but accumulate in landfills and oceans, harming wildlife and ecosystems.

## DDT (as pesticide)

Image: ../images/ddt.png

Effective at killing pests but caused environmental damage (bioaccumulation) and harms to birds and other species.

## Fossil-Fuel Dependency (Coal, Oil) Combustion

Image: ../images/fossil_fuels.png

Reliable energy sources but their widespread burning releases greenhouse gases driving climate change and air pollution.

## Unregulated Industrial Waste Disposal

Image: ../images/industrial_waste.png

Industrial processes that dumped toxic waste into rivers and ground caused contaminated water, soil, and long-term public health crises.

## Nuclear Weapons

Image: ../images/nuclear_weapons.png

Weapons with catastrophic destructive power and long-lasting environmental, human, and political consequences.
