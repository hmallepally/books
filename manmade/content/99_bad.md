# Bad: Problematic Inventions

## Asbestos Insulation

Image: ../images/asbestos.png

Asbestos was widely used for insulation and fireproofing. Its fibers cause severe lung disease and cancer when inhaled. Once hailed as a miracle material, it's now known to be highly hazardous.

## Leaded Gasoline

Image: ../images/leaded_gasoline.png

Adding lead to gasoline increased engine performance but released toxic lead into the environment, causing widespread health problems. It took many decades to phase out leaded fuel.
