standardization of processes, and continuous learning. Unlike traditional improvement approaches that focus

on large, dramatic changes, Kaizen promotes a culture where small improvements are made continuously,

The Discovery: The bias wasn't intentional, but it was devastating in its impact. The AI system had

The Response: Sarah immediately halted the system's deployment and led a comprehensive review

fairness constraints that actively worked against historical biases, diversified their training data to

include more representative samples, and created ongoing monitoring systems that would catch

similar issues before they could cause harm.

The revised system not only eliminated bias but actually improved outcomes for underserved

populations. By actively working to counteract historical inequalities, the AI system became a force
